Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account? +

You can delete your account by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, matches, and messages will be permanently removed.

How do I report inappropriate behavior? +

You can report inappropriate behavior by clicking the "Report" button on any profile or message. You can also email us directly at safety@lavrilo.com. We take all reports seriously and investigate them promptly.

Why am I not getting matches? +

There could be several reasons: incomplete profile, limited search criteria, or location settings. Try updating your profile with more photos and information, expanding your search preferences, or adjusting your location radius.

How does the matching algorithm work? +

Our AI-powered algorithm analyzes over 200 compatibility factors including interests, values, lifestyle preferences, and behavioral patterns to suggest the most compatible matches for you.

Is my personal information secure? +

Yes, we use bank-level encryption and security measures to protect your data. We never share your personal information with third parties without your consent. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I cancel my subscription? +

You can cancel your subscription anytime in Settings > Subscription. Your premium features will remain active until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are provided for partial periods.
