FAQ for Journalists

What makes Lavrilo different from other dating platforms?

Lavrilo focuses on authentic connections through advanced compatibility matching, comprehensive safety measures, and a user-centric approach that prioritizes meaningful relationships over casual encounters.

What are your current user statistics?

We have 40,000+ active profiles, 1,300+ daily online users, and over 10,000 documented success stories. Our platform maintains a strong focus on quality connections rather than inflated user numbers.

How does your safety system work?

Our multi-layer verification system includes photo verification, profile moderation, and AI-powered detection of suspicious behavior. We maintain a 24/7 support team and have partnerships with safety organizations.

What is your approach to user privacy?

We implement end-to-end encryption for messages, minimal data collection policies, and give users full control over their privacy settings. We never sell user data to third parties.
