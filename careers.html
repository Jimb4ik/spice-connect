Our Culture

At Lavrilo, we believe that great relationships start with great teams. We foster an inclusive, collaborative environment where everyone can thrive:

  • Diversity & Inclusion: We celebrate different perspectives and backgrounds
  • Innovation: We encourage experimentation and learning from failures
  • Work-Life Balance: We respect personal time and promote mental health
  • Transparency: Open communication and regular feedback are core to our culture
  • Impact: Every team member contributes to helping people find meaningful connections

Application Process

Our hiring process is designed to be fair, transparent, and respectful of your time:

  1. Application Review: We review all applications within 5 business days
  2. Initial Screen: 30-minute video call with our talent team
  3. Technical/Portfolio Review: Role-specific assessment or portfolio presentation
  4. Team Interview: Meet with potential teammates and hiring manager
  5. Final Interview: Culture fit and leadership discussion
  6. Offer: We aim to make decisions within 48 hours of final interview

We're committed to providing feedback at every stage and ensuring a positive candidate experience, regardless of the outcome.
